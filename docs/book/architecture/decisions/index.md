## Context

The workspace is a new get-started scaffold with no committed design
decisions beyond the target direction in
[Technology targets](../technology.md). Without a central place to track
future decisions, implementation choices risk being made ad hoc and becoming
hard to reverse.

## Decision

Record each significant future decision as an architecture decision record
(ADR) that follows the [ADR template](template.md) and captures context, the
chosen direction, consequences, alternatives, and related work.

## Consequences

- Future decisions stay visible to the whole team before implementation starts.
- New decisions can be proposed using a single template and review checklist.
- Accepted ADRs become part of the handbook and are updated or superseded when
  the project learns more.
