# ADR-021: Environment visuals — exposure, per-regime depth cueing, zodiacal light

- **Status:** binding (2026-09-17 — zodiacal-light section landed with
  `plans/v0.2.0/zodiacal-light`: `engine::render::zodiacal` analytic
  model + `ZodiacalSource` interface; exposure section landed with
  `plans/v0.2.0/exposure-tone-mapping`: `engine::render::exposure`
  kernel + HDR/ACES post chain; proposed by navigation-engine spec
  [`cosmic-navigation-engine-v0.4.md`](../techstack/cosmic-navigation-engine-v0.4.md)
  §9; `plans/v0.2.0/depth-cueing` implements its section under it)

## Context

Sun vs background starlight spans ~10 orders of magnitude; surface
daylight vs deep space ~30 stops. No fixed exposure covers both. Classic
atmospheric haze is physically valid only inside an atmosphere — every
vacuum regime needs a physically valid substitute depth cue.

## Decision

- **Exposure:** physically driven auto-exposure keyed to the dominant
  light source in view (Sun, planet albedo, ambient starlight); filmic
  tone mapping (ACES or comparable), never clipping; gradual dark
  adaptation — stars fade in across the effective scotopic threshold
  (civil/nautical/astronomical twilight), no popping at a fixed altitude.
- **Per-regime depth cueing (spec §9.1):** zodiacal dust glow
  (interplanetary); dust extinction/reddening `E(B−V)`, `Aᵥ`
  (interstellar); extinction + peculiar-velocity Doppler tinting (local
  intergalactic — cosmological redshift is **not** monotonic there, cf.
  blueshifted Andromeda); cosmological redshift + raymarched seeded
  volumetric density fields (cosmic web); real Rayleigh/Mie scattering
  only at atmosphere waypoints 7–10.
- **Zodiacal light (spec §9.4):** analytic model in the solar-system
  frame — μ₀ = 23.0 mag/arcsec² at the ecliptic poles, magnitude-offset
  form with ecliptic-latitude falloff (Aβ = 1.5 mag, β₀ = 20°) and
  forward-scattering phase term (Aθ = 0.5 mag, k = 0.7); converted to
  linear radiance before exposure/tone mapping. Replacement path to a
  map-based HEALPix asset must not change render interfaces. Validate
  against COBE/DIRBE or HST background references before release.

## Consequences

- Waypoint-to-waypoint transition experiences (spec §9.3) are built on
  these primitives — owned by `plans/v0.2.0/waypoint-transitions`.
- The visual/rendering-style open item (PBR vs stylized, spec §10) can
  tune parameters but not replace the physical basis decided here.
