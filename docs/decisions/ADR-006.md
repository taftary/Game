# ADR-006: Audio backend — deferred to navigation-engine v0.5

- **Status:** accepted (2026-09-17, PO decision recorded in
  navigation-engine spec
  [`cosmic-navigation-engine-v0.4.md`](../techstack/cosmic-navigation-engine-v0.4.md)
  §10)

## Context

The navigation-engine spec must fix what is in and out of the v0.4 scope.
Audio is a full backend decision (device management, mixing, spatialization)
with no consumer in the current milestone set.

## Decision

- Audio is **deferred to spec v0.5**. The v0.4 scope ships silent except
  for platform-required accessibility/system feedback, if any.
- The renderer and simulation **must not depend on an audio clock** —
  no module may assume an audio device exists.

## Consequences

- No audio backend is selected yet; this ADR's "backend choice" part
  reopens when spec v0.5 is drafted.
- Quality gates and perf budgets stay audio-free until then.
