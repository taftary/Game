# ADR-014: Sphere-of-influence handoff — soft patched-conic blending

- **Status:** draft (2026-09-17, proposed by navigation-engine spec
  [`cosmic-navigation-engine-v0.4.md`](../techstack/cosmic-navigation-engine-v0.4.md)
  §3; binding when `plans/v0.1.0/soi-handoff` lands)

## Context

A ship crossing between gravitational domains must switch the dominant
frame (ADR-012) without the player seeing a jump. Hard SOI switches cause
velocity kicks and HUD flicker.

## Decision

- **Soft patched-conic handoff:** position C⁰ continuous, velocity
  approximately C¹ continuous. Transform position and velocity state
  vectors **before** the physics step that crosses the boundary, then
  blend acceleration contributions across a radial band.
- Eligibility begins when the candidate secondary's acceleration ≥
  10⁻³ of the primary's.
- Blend band: 10% radial band around the nominal Laplace SOI radius
  (1.05 × r_soi outside → 0.95 × r_soi inside), **smoothstep** weighting
  (no linear ramp, avoids an acceleration-derivative kink).
- Nominal radius: Laplace `r_SOI = a·(m/M)^(2/5)`. For **stability
  analysis** (moon orbit limits, ring boundaries) use the Hill sphere
  `r_Hill = a·(m/3M)^(1/3)` instead — typically 0.5–0.7 × Laplace.
- Player-observable requirement: no position jump, velocity kick, camera
  discontinuity, or HUD flicker.

## Consequences

- HUD must surface the blend band (spec §10: indicator with hysteresis,
  "Approaching / Entering [body] SOI" above blend weight 0.2) —
  consumed by `plans/v0.3.0/navigation-hud`.
- Autosave triggers on completed handoffs (ADR-004).
